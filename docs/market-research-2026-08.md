# Market signals for v0.2

Observed 2026-08-23. Marketplace counts are demand signals, not quality approval.

## SkillHub enterprise zone

The public enterprise endpoints show strong supply and download signals around office documents, knowledge search, email, content marketing, tender/company intelligence, lead discovery and professional document review.

- Enterprise zone: https://skillhub.cn/enterprise-zone
- Featured enterprises: https://api.skillhub.cn/api/v1/enterprises/featured?pageSize=10
- Hot enterprise Skills: https://api.skillhub.cn/api/v1/enterprises/hot-skills?pageSize=20
- Industry taxonomy: https://api.skillhub.cn/api/v1/enterprises/industry-tags

XOPC conclusion: prioritize complete business outcomes, while independently reviewing upstream source, license, permissions and evaluation quality.

## ClawHub

ClawHub emphasizes app-connected work across GitHub, Notion, Slack, Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Calendar, Linear and Figma, alongside productivity and automation Skills: https://clawhub.ai/

XOPC conclusion: a useful Skill should separate reasoning from external writes and require confirmation before messages, tasks, calendar changes or paid calls.

## skills.sh and VoltAgent

- skills.sh leaderboard: https://www.skills.sh/
- VoltAgent curated index: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills

The mature end of the ecosystem remains strong in software delivery, research, design and artifact creation. XOPC uses these indexes for discovery, then pins and reviews original repositories. Popularity or inclusion alone is insufficient.
