# Personal Budget Review

Confirm household members included, period, currency, income variability, shared versus personal expenses, goals, known obligations, and privacy preferences. Minimize account numbers, identity fields, and transaction descriptions before analysis.

Reconcile opening balance, income, transfers, spending, debt payments, and closing balance when possible. Separate transfers from expenses, refunds from income, committed from discretionary spending, and monthly costs from annual or irregular obligations. Keep uncertain categories visible.

Use a trailing period that reflects seasonality. Report ranges for variable income and costs. Build a baseline cash-flow view, recurring commitments, category trends, upcoming sinking funds, and goal gap. Test a base, constrained, and disruption scenario without assuming future income.

Deliver a private transaction exception list, monthly baseline, upcoming irregular expenses, goal scenarios, a small set of user-chosen actions, and review cadence. State assumptions and leave consequential choices to the user. Do not connect, transfer, cancel, borrow, invest, or disclose records without explicit authorization.
