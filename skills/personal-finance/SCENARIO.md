# Personal finance

## User outcome

Turn household cash-flow records and goals into a privacy-aware budget review with explicit assumptions and feasible actions.

## Selected Skills

- `personal-budget-review` owns transaction categorization, recurring cash-flow analysis, sinking funds, scenarios, and a user-controlled budget plan.

## Boundary

This scenario does not provide investment, credit, tax, insurance, or debt-relief professional advice and never moves money or changes accounts.
