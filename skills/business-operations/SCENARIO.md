# Business operations

## User outcome

Turn bounded customer or financial intake into a reviewable operational decision package without silently changing systems of record.

## Selected Skills

- `customer-support-ticket-triage` owns customer issue classification, impact, routing, and initial-response draft.
- `invoice-receipt-reconciliation` owns financial document extraction, matching, and exception review.
- `sop-authoring` owns observed-process mapping, role and control definition, exception paths, procedure testing, and controlled rollout.

## Boundary

These Skills prepare evidence and recommendations. Ticket updates, customer messages, accounting entries, approvals, payments, file mutations, and controlled SOP publication require explicit authorization and appropriate domain review.
