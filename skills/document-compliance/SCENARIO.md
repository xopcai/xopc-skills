# Document compliance

## User outcome

Trace a supplied document against supplied requirements and produce an atomic evidence matrix with `met`, `partial`, `not met`, and `not verifiable` states.

## Selected Skill

- `document-requirements-review` — adapted from [Mohit Aggarwal's PM skills](https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills). XOPC narrowed it from broad compliance guidance to evidence-based document review and removed certification or legal-advice claims.

## Boundary and overlap

The Skill never invents governing requirements, certifies compliance, or substitutes for regulated professional judgment. It reviews only the requirement set and document supplied or explicitly authorized by the user.

## Evaluated alternatives

- Legal-document analyzers and quality-management document Skills address different risk regimes and were not merged into this general traceability task.
- Regulated-domain Skills may be added only with named authoritative sources, qualified review, dedicated fixtures, and a non-overlapping trigger.
