# Decision research

## User outcome

Turn a consequential question into a decision-ready brief with explicit criteria, independent evidence, contradictions, confidence, and unresolved risks.

## Selected Skill

- `evidence-based-research` — adapted from [Microsoft vscode-team-kit](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-team-kit). It was selected for first-party engineering provenance and a strong research workflow, then narrowed to a portable decision artifact with citation and stopping rules.

## Boundary and overlap

This scenario does not own prospect lists, generic web summaries, literature-only reviews, or the downstream business deliverable. Research ends at supported findings and a decision brief.

## Evaluated alternatives

- Community deep-research Skills remain watchlist candidates. They were not added because a second general research Skill would duplicate the same trigger before demonstrating a measurable quality advantage.
- Add a separate Skill only for a distinct evidence regime, such as systematic academic review, with different sources and acceptance tests.
