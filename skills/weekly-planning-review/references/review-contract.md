# Weekly review contract

## Inputs

Review period, timezone, workdays, task/project sources, calendar constraints, waiting items, protected time, effort estimates, and any measured estimate-to-actual ratio.

## Capacity

Usable capacity = available working time − fixed meetings/duties − protected constraints − buffer. Apply the user's measured estimate ratio when enough samples exist; otherwise label estimates as provisional.

## Required dispositions

Every overdue item: do next, reschedule with reason, delegate with follow-up, reduce scope, drop, or explicitly defer. Every active project: one physical next action or hold/drop. Every waiting item: follow-up date or drop.

## Deliverable

Include wins, unresolved commitments, capacity calculation, chosen outcomes, explicit cuts, calendar proposal, waiting follow-ups, risks, and next review date.
