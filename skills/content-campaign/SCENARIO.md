# Content campaign

## User outcome

Turn a business goal and audience evidence into a coherent campaign package: content pillars, asset briefs, channel adaptations, claims lineage, and measurable success criteria.

## Selected Skill

- `content-campaign-pack` — adapted from [Corey Haines' Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), a widely adopted MIT-licensed marketing Skill collection. Its content-strategy foundation was extended into an end-to-end, approval-safe campaign artifact.

## Boundary and overlap

This Skill plans and specifies a multi-asset campaign. It does not own single-asset copywriting, paid-media buying, autonomous publishing, visual generation, or customer research.

## Evaluated alternatives

- The same upstream's copywriting, social-content, and email Skills are strong future candidates only if added as non-overlapping execution steps consuming the campaign brief.
- Generic marketing-operations Skills were not copied because they overlap the campaign trigger and broaden permissions without improving this outcome.
