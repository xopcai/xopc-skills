# Content Campaign Pack

Create a coherent, executable campaign—not a pile of disconnected drafts.

## Workflow

1. Establish the business outcome, audience, offer, buyer stage, channels, timeframe, resources, brand constraints, evidence available, and prohibited claims. Reuse provided product/customer context rather than interviewing again.
2. Extract audience questions, pains, objections, desired outcomes, and exact language from supplied research, calls, support data, and analytics. Label assumptions where evidence is absent.
3. Choose 3–5 content pillars tied to audience needs and product relevance. Classify each idea as demand capture, demand creation, or both.
4. Prioritize ideas using customer impact, content–market fit, discoverability/distribution, and production effort. Show input evidence and avoid invented search volumes or conversion forecasts.
5. Design one campaign narrative with a core claim, proof, CTA, objections, and content sequence. Read [references/campaign-contract.md](references/campaign-contract.md).
6. Produce asset briefs before copy: purpose, audience, angle, source facts, required proof, format, channel, CTA, owner, due date, and acceptance criteria.
7. Create channel adaptations from the approved core message. Preserve facts and meaning while changing hook, length, structure, and CTA. Do not pretend one identical post is channel strategy.
8. Run fact, brand, privacy, accessibility, and unsupported-claim checks. Deliver a calendar and measurement plan with leading indicators, decision thresholds, and review date.

## Deliverables

- Campaign brief and audience evidence
- Pillars and prioritized idea backlog
- Core narrative and message hierarchy
- Asset briefs and selected draft variants
- Channel calendar with owners/dependencies
- Claim/evidence register
- Measurement and iteration plan

## Hard boundaries

- Never promise virality, rankings, leads, or revenue.
- Do not invent customer quotes, testimonials, performance metrics, or competitor claims.
- Do not publish, schedule, spend ad budget, or message an audience without explicit authorization.
- A request for one article or one social post should use a narrower writing capability, not this Skill.
