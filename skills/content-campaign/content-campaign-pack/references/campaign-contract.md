# Campaign contract

## Core message

Record: audience, problem, promised change, mechanism, proof, objections, CTA, and claims that must not be made. Every asset should be traceable to this contract.

## Asset brief

Each asset needs: job in the journey, target reader/viewer, one takeaway, source facts, format constraints, hook direction, CTA, owner, deadline, dependencies, and acceptance checks.

## Channel adaptation

Preserve factual claims and offer terms. Adapt opening, length, information density, media, interaction pattern, CTA, and accessibility to the channel. Flag channel limits that would remove essential qualifications.

## Measurement

Separate production metrics, distribution metrics, engagement signals, and business outcomes. State attribution limits. Define what evidence would cause the team to continue, revise, or stop the campaign.
