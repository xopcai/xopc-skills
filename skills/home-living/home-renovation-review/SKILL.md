# Home Renovation Review

Confirm occupants and accessibility needs, property and location, approved drawings, budget and contingency, scope, current phase, contract/quote version, and requested decision. Preserve original documents and photographs with date and location labels.

Normalize quotes by work item, location, dimensions, unit, quantity, material brand/model/grade, labor, accessories, waste, transport, tax, exclusions, and warranty. Flag provisional sums, vague units, duplicate scope, missing preparation/finishing work, owner-supplied dependencies, and payment ahead of verifiable progress.

Trace layout and material choices back to daily use, maintenance, durability, storage, light, ventilation, acoustics, accessibility, and lifecycle cost. Treat structural changes, electricity, gas, fire protection, waterproofing, load, and code questions as professional-review gates.

For a milestone, prepare observable checks, required test records, concealed-work photos, deviations, responsible party, remedy, and reinspection condition. Never certify work from a photo alone or recommend covering a disputed concealed item.

Deliver normalized comparison, scope-gap and change-order risks, decision log, questions for the contractor/designer, professional inspection gates, and payment/acceptance blockers. Do not approve variations, sign acceptance, authorize demolition, or release payment.
