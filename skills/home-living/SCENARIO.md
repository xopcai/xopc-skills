# Home living

## User outcome
Review a home-renovation plan, quote, or milestone through traceable requirements, budget, quality, and professional-review gates.

## Selected Skills
- `home-renovation-review` owns needs, quote normalization, scope gaps, milestone evidence, change control, and inspection preparation.

## Boundary
This Skill cannot certify structural, electrical, gas, waterproofing, fire, or code safety and does not replace licensed designers, engineers, inspectors, or legal review.
