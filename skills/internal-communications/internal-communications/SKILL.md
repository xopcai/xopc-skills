# Internal Communications

Start from verified facts, the intended audience, the decision or response needed, and the reporting period. If the user supplies a company template or voice guide, follow it; otherwise choose the lightest format that makes the update useful.

## Choose the form

- **Status or 3P update:** progress, next plans, problems or decisions needed.
- **Leadership brief:** outcome, business impact, risk, decision, owner, and date.
- **Incident update:** current impact, known facts, actions underway, next update time; never speculate about cause.
- **FAQ:** answer the actual stakeholder questions consistently, separating confirmed policy from pending decisions.
- **Newsletter:** prioritize material changes and useful calls to action over activity volume.

## Drafting rules

Separate facts, estimates, decisions, risks, and asks. Preserve uncertainty and attribution. Do not turn activity into claimed impact, omit material blockers, expose sensitive personal or security details, or invent owners and deadlines. Use links or source labels supplied by the user so critical claims can be checked.

Return a ready-to-review draft plus a short list of missing facts and assumptions. Sending or publishing is a separate action requiring explicit confirmation of audience and final text.
