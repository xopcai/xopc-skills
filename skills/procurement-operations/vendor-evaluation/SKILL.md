# Vendor Evaluation

Define the decision, mandatory requirements, evaluation period, stakeholders, alternatives, and weighting before scoring. Separate vendor claims, contractual commitments, customer evidence, internal experience, and analyst inference.

Build total cost of ownership from license or usage fees, implementation, integration, migration, training, support, operations, growth assumptions, and exit costs. Keep currencies, tax treatment, and one-time versus recurring costs explicit. Do not fabricate missing pricing or force incomparable offers into false precision.

Assess functional fit, integration and data portability, security and compliance evidence, reliability and support, financial or concentration risk, business continuity, lock-in, contract terms, and implementation capacity. Score only where evidence supports it and run sensitivity checks when weights materially change the recommendation.

Return an executive recommendation, requirements traceability, TCO assumptions, comparison matrix, risk register with mitigations and owners, evidence gaps, negotiation points, and next due-diligence actions.

Legal interpretation, security certification, procurement approval, contacting vendors, signing, purchasing, or contract changes require qualified review and explicit authorization.
