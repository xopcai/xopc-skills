# Business Budget Variance

Align entity, department, accounting period, fiscal calendar, currency, unit and accounting basis before comparison. Identify the approved budget version, forecast version and actuals extraction date. Preserve the original baseline when constructing a new forecast.

Map budget and actual accounts explicitly. Keep unmapped lines, missing records, intercompany items, one-offs, accruals and currency effects visible. Missing actuals are unknown rather than zero. Separate year-to-date from single-period data and reconcile mapped totals to each source.

## Calculate and explain
Use actual minus budget as the signed variance and state that convention. Show percentage variance as (actual minus budget) / absolute budget when the budget is nonzero; use N/A for a zero baseline. Flag negative-baseline interpretation. Determine favorable or unfavorable by metric meaning: higher revenue can be favorable; higher comparable expense can be unfavorable. A lower expense caused by missed delivery is not automatically a business improvement.

Where quantities and comparable unit rates exist, an exact bridge can use volume effect = (actual quantity - budget quantity) × budget rate and rate effect = actual quantity × (actual rate - budget rate). State this convention so the interaction is not counted twice. For multiple products or currencies, separate mix and FX only when inputs support the decomposition. Reconcile driver contributions to total variance.

Distinguish measured drivers from management hypotheses. Rank by absolute impact, repeated deviation and decision relevance using a stated materiality threshold. Do not manufacture benchmark targets or causal explanations.

## Forecast and deliver
Build any requested forecast from actuals to date plus explicit remaining-period assumptions. Label scenarios and sensitivities; do not replace missing data with a claimed prediction. Reconcile the forecast and avoid mixing actuals with budget periods twice.

Deliver a source and mapping note, budget/actual/variance table, reconciled driver bridge and actions with owner, evidence needed and review date. Recalculate numerical results with available tools. Keep raw payroll and account identifiers restricted. Accounting policy changes, tax treatment, payments and ledger postings remain outside this analytical task.
