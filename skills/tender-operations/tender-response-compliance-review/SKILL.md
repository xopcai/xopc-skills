# Tender Response Compliance Review

Confirm the controlling tender version, submission deadline and channel, lots, bidder identity, review scope, and available response files. Preserve originals and record source page or section for every extracted requirement.

Build a requirement matrix covering eligibility, mandatory clauses, technical and commercial response, scoring evidence, forms, signatures and seals, authorization, dates, file naming, format, copies, encryption, submission sequence, and clarification history. Separate explicit requirement, interpretation, response location, evidence, status, owner, and due action.

Flag unconditional or fatal language exactly as written, but do not label a bid legally invalid without qualified review. Detect omissions, contradictions, expired evidence, entity-name mismatch, unsupported claims, unpriced items, unauthorized deviations, and requirements acknowledged without proof. Never invent certificates, performance history, staff credentials, signatures, seals, dates, or customer evidence.

When current law, procurement rules, or platform instructions matter, verify dated official sources and distinguish them from the tender's own terms. Deliver the matrix, blocker list, questions for the issuer or counsel, completion owners, and a final pre-submission checklist. Do not upload or submit without explicit authorization.
