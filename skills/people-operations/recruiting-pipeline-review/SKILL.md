# Recruiting Pipeline Review

Confirm the requisitions, reporting period, canonical stage definitions, owners, service expectations, and permitted candidate fields. Minimize personal data and exclude protected or irrelevant attributes from evaluation.

Normalize candidate and requisition identities, deduplicate records, and distinguish current stage from historical events. Calculate stage counts, aging, conversion, time in stage, source performance, interview completion, feedback gaps, offer state, and time to fill only when definitions and denominators are available.

Flag stalled candidates, missing structured feedback, inconsistent stage use, scheduling bottlenecks, expiring offers, and capacity constraints. Recommendations must be tied to job criteria and recorded evidence, not names, photos, schools, demographic proxies, personality guesses, or protected traits.

Return scope and data quality, pipeline summary by requisition, bottlenecks and aging, metric definitions, open decisions, owners and next actions, and fairness or privacy concerns. Suppress small-group breakdowns that could expose individuals.

Moving stages, rejecting candidates, contacting people, changing requisitions, compensation decisions, or final hiring decisions require authorized human review and explicit confirmation.
