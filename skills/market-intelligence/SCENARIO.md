# Market intelligence

## User outcome

Turn current primary and independent evidence into a fair, decision-specific competitor comparison and monitoring plan.

## Selected Skills

- `competitive-intelligence` owns competitor selection, source triangulation, dated comparison, evidence confidence, implications, and update signals.

## Boundary

General decision research remains in `evidence-based-research`, prospect lists in `prospect-research`, and ecommerce store diagnostics in `ecommerce-performance-diagnosis`. This scenario does not use deception, restricted data, or invented win/loss claims.
