# Business analysis

## User outcome

Turn business records into decision-ready financial or ecommerce operating insight without hiding data quality limits.

## Selected Skills

- `financial-statement-analysis` owns multi-period statement normalization, ratio analysis, cash-flow quality, and management-facing financial diagnostics.
- `ecommerce-performance-diagnosis` owns platform-neutral GMV, traffic, conversion, product, customer, fulfillment, and margin diagnosis for Chinese ecommerce operations.

## Boundary

Workbook construction remains in `spreadsheet-workbench`, chart production in `data-visualization-report`, and invoice matching in `invoice-receipt-reconciliation`. These Skills analyze supplied business evidence; they do not audit accounts, file taxes, value securities, or operate a store.
