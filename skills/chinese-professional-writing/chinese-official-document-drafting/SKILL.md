# Chinese Official Document Drafting

First determine the sender, recipient, purpose, authority relationship, requested action, deadline, confidentiality level, and approval state. Ask for the governing policy, facts, names, dates, figures, attachments, and house style. If the document type is uncertain, explain the plausible choices and their consequences rather than guessing.

## Select the document shape

Use the current official rules and organization template supplied by the user. Verify time-sensitive standards against authoritative government sources when browsing is available. Separate statutory party-and-government document types from internal reports, briefings, speeches, work summaries, and news-style materials.

Check direction and intent:

- request approval or instruction with a clear ask and no mixed unrelated asks;
- report facts and progress without disguising a request as a report;
- communicate requirements with scope, responsible parties, timing, and implementation details;
- record a meeting by decisions and responsibilities, not by invented consensus;
- correspond across non-subordinate organizations with respectful, bounded language.

## Draft from evidence

Build a fact sheet before prose. Mark every policy citation, statistic, quotation, title, organization name, and decision as verified, user-provided, or pending confirmation. Never invent a document number, approval, leader quotation, policy clause, meeting decision, or performance figure.

Use direct Chinese, stable terminology, parallel structure only where it improves comprehension, and concrete verbs for responsibility. Remove empty slogans, redundant headings, exaggerated achievements, vague subjects, and commitments without an owner or date. Preserve politically or legally sensitive wording exactly when it comes from an approved source.

## Review before delivery

Review purpose, document-type fit, addressee relationship, title, opening basis, logical hierarchy, facts, citations, responsibilities, dates, attachment references, confidentiality, and sign-off placeholders. Check that a request can be answered, a notice can be executed, and minutes distinguish decisions from discussion.

Deliver:

- the clean draft;
- a short document-type and structure rationale;
- a verification list for missing or time-sensitive facts;
- a change note when revising an existing draft;
- optional layout handoff requirements for a document-rendering Skill.

Use visible placeholders such as `【待确认：发文机关】` only for genuinely missing user facts. Do not send, stamp, number, or publish the document without explicit authorization and the organization's formal approval process.
