# Chinese professional writing

## User outcome

Draft or revise Chinese official and administrative documents with the correct purpose, relationship, structure, evidence, and approval boundary.

## Selected Skills

- `chinese-official-document-drafting` owns Chinese 党政机关/国企/事业单位公文 and administrative-text content structure, language, references, and compliance review.

## Boundary

General editable-document layout and rendering remain in `document-authoring`. This scenario does not impersonate an issuing authority, invent approvals, choose a legally operative document type without context, or publish a document.
