# Chinese Natural Style Editing

Confirm audience, purpose, desired voice, allowed rewrite depth, terms that must remain, and whether comments or a clean draft are needed. If no voice sample is supplied, make restrained edits instead of inventing a persona.

Preserve facts, numbers, quotations, citations, commitments, and uncertainty. Mark any substantive change separately. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs, varied sentence length, direct transitions, and paragraph logic over mechanical synonym replacement. Remove empty framing, repeated conclusions, unsupported grandeur, excessive headings, false contrasts, and formulaic summaries only when they weaken the text.

Do not add personal anecdotes, emotions, credentials, interviews, data, or sources the author did not provide. Do not claim that revised text is human-authored or guaranteed to pass an AI detector. For academic, legal, medical, financial, or official content, retain traceability and route domain claims to the relevant review.

Deliver the revised text plus a short change note covering voice, structure, and any claim that needs author verification. When the requested style conflicts with accuracy or authorship, preserve the truth and explain the limit.
