# Chinese content editing

## User outcome
Revise supplied Chinese content into clear, natural language that preserves facts, intent, and the author's recognizable voice.

## Selected Skills
- `chinese-natural-style-editing` owns sentence rhythm, specificity, structure, voice, and minimal natural-language revision.

## Boundary
This scenario does not promise AI-detector evasion, invent personal experience, hide plagiarism, or replace factual and citation review.
