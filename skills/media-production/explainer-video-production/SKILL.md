# Explainer Video Production

Define the audience, single learning outcome, source of truth, target duration, aspect ratio, channel, language, brand constraints, and production capabilities. Separate supplied facts from claims requiring research; cite or flag important claims instead of making the script sound certain.

Build a concise narrative: hook, problem or context, explanation in digestible steps, evidence or example, and closing action. Convert it into timed scenes with narration, on-screen text, visual direction, transitions, and asset provenance. Keep on-screen text readable and do not make narration compete with dense visuals.

Select only authorized voices, music, footage, fonts, logos, and generated assets. Disclose synthetic media where required. Any paid generation, external upload, or licensed-asset purchase requires approval. Do not clone a person's voice or likeness without their explicit authorization.

Produce through the tools actually available, or deliver a production-ready package when rendering is unavailable. Verify facts, pronunciation, pacing, caption accuracy, audio levels, safe margins, aspect ratio, duration, visual continuity, and a full final render rather than isolated frames.

Return the objective and audience, approved script, timed storyboard, asset and rights manifest, production settings, output locations, QA evidence, limitations, and publishing handoff. Publishing remains a separate authorized action.
