# Media production

## User outcome

Turn source media or trusted information into a reviewable transcript or one coherent explainer video without hiding uncertainty, rights, privacy, or production limitations.

## Selected Skills

- `audio-transcription` owns faithful, time-addressable speech-to-text.
- `explainer-video-production` owns the script-to-render explanatory video package.

## Boundary

Meeting synthesis belongs to `meeting-to-actions`; broad campaigns belong to `content-campaign-pack`; presentations belong to `presentation-deck`. Uploads, paid generation, voice or likeness use, and publishing require explicit authorization.
