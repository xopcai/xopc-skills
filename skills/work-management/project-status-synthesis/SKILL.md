# Project Status Synthesis

Confirm the project, reporting period, audience, and comparison baseline. Gather the smallest useful evidence set: milestones, committed scope, metric definitions and values, completed and active work, blockers, risks, decisions, and prior-period status.

Reconcile identities and dates across sources. Distinguish completed, in progress, blocked, and unverified work. A missing signal is unknown, not green. Assign an overall health only when criteria or sufficient evidence exist; state the basis and use the organization's definitions when supplied.

Lead with the decision-relevant headline. Include:

- reporting scope and evidence cutoff;
- overall health with rationale and confidence;
- target, actual, trend, and source for material metrics;
- outcomes completed and meaningful work in progress;
- risks or issues with impact, owner, mitigation, and next check;
- decisions needed with owner and deadline;
- next-period priorities tied to capacity or dependencies;
- data gaps and conflicting evidence.

Do not count activity as progress, fabricate targets, or conceal a slipped milestone behind a green aggregate. Drafting or posting a stakeholder message is separate; updating trackers or metrics requires explicit authorization.
