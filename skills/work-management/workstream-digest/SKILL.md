# Workstream Digest

Require a bounded scope: named channels, threads, topics, projects, or teams plus a time window and timezone. Never guess the user's important channels or expand to organization-wide monitoring without a clear request.

Read enough parent and reply context to avoid treating an intermediate comment as the outcome. Prioritize decisions, blockers, incidents, explicit asks, ownership or deadline changes, and meaningful status changes. Exclude greetings, repeated notifications, low-signal reactions, and unchanged routine activity.

Group by topic or workstream, not mechanically by source. For each material item include what changed, why it matters, owner or audience when known, and a source pointer. Keep unresolved questions separate from confirmed decisions.

Return scope and coverage first, then a short summary, grouped updates, items needing attention, and gaps or omitted sources. State partial access and sparse coverage. Posting, replying, or notifying people is a separate authorized action.

Do not infer silence as completion, manufacture urgency, or claim comprehensive coverage when pagination, retention, access, or search limits prevented it.
