# China Social Content Adaptation

Confirm platform, account identity, audience, objective, source material, brand voice, format limits, commercial relationship, rights, and desired call to action. Verify current platform rules when they materially affect the asset.

Create a fact sheet and claims ledger before writing. Preserve qualifications and cite or link claims as the format permits. Never invent personal experience, customer testimony, test results, scarcity, prices, endorsements, or trending status.

Adapt one idea to the platform's reading or viewing behavior without copying another creator's voice: structure, opening, density, visual beats, title, caption, tags, and interaction prompt should serve the audience and content, not exploit misleading clickbait. Mark advertising, sponsorship, AI-generated media, and regulated claims where required.

Deliver the final copy or script, title options, visual/shot brief when requested, claims and rights checklist, platform-format check, and publication preview. Remove hidden personal information and confirm links, names, numbers, dates, cover crop, subtitles, and disclosures. Publishing, scheduling, account login, comments, and paid promotion require separate explicit authorization.
