# Teaching support

## User outcome

Create a teachable, standards-aware lesson plan in which objectives, activities, assessment, timing, and learner differences align.

## Selected Skills

- `teaching-plan-design` owns lesson-level instructional design, objective-assessment alignment, classroom flow, differentiation, and teacher-ready checks.

## Boundary

Presentation production remains in `presentation-deck`, general document rendering in `document-authoring`, and broad evidence research in `evidence-based-research`. The Skill supports teachers and does not claim official curriculum approval.
