# Teaching support

## User outcome

Create a teachable, standards-aware lesson plan in which objectives, activities, assessment, timing, and learner differences align.

## Selected Skills

- `teaching-plan-design` owns lesson-level instructional design, objective-assessment alignment, classroom flow, differentiation, and teacher-ready checks.
- `learning-assessment-analysis` owns assessment-data validation, item and objective analysis, misconception evidence, instructional grouping, and reassessment planning.

## Boundary

Presentation production remains in `presentation-deck`, general document rendering in `document-authoring`, and broad evidence research in `evidence-based-research`. Lesson creation belongs to `teaching-plan-design`; analysis of existing learner evidence belongs to `learning-assessment-analysis`. Neither claims official curriculum approval.
