# Commitment and decision rules

## Commitment

A commitment needs an actor plus a concrete future action. “Priya will send the spec Friday” is explicit. “We should send the spec” is an orphan. “Priya can send it” is capability, not agreement.

## Due dates

Normalize a relative date only when the meeting date and timezone are known. Preserve the original phrase alongside the normalized date. Otherwise use `UNKNOWN`.

## Decisions

Decision signals include explicit approval, selection, rejection, or commitment by the authorized participants. Brainstormed options, unanswered questions, and one participant's preference remain discussion.

## Evidence

For traceability, record a short paraphrase plus timestamp/line/section when available. Avoid long transcript quotes and omit sensitive tangents.

## Orphans

Keep unowned ideas separate. Ask the user to assign or drop them; never silently convert them into team tasks.
