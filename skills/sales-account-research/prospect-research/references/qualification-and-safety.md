# Qualification and safety

## Evidence tiers

- High: official company source plus an independent current source, or two authoritative independent sources.
- Medium: one credible current source with consistent supporting signals.
- Low: indirect, incomplete, stale, or ambiguous evidence. Low-confidence rows cannot be top priority.

## Fit and timing

Score separately. Fit covers firmographic, technographic, geographic, use-case, and disqualifier criteria. Timing covers explicit initiatives, hiring, funding, product change, vendor event, public pain, or another dated trigger relevant to the offer.

## Contacts

Prefer published business channels and role addresses. Store provenance and observed date. A third-party enrichment result is not verified identity or consent; follow the provider's terms and applicable law.

## Output hygiene

Remove duplicates, disclose coverage gaps, include rejected-count reasons, and never hide that a target count could not be met at the requested quality bar.
