# Sales account research

## User outcome

Convert an ICP into a verified, explainable prospect list using company fit, timing signals, source lineage, and uncertainty.

## Selected Skill

- `prospect-research` — adapted from the `prospecting` Skill in [Corey Haines' Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills). It is the strongest reviewed open-source fit for this exact task and was tightened around privacy, enrichment, and evidence boundaries.

## Boundary and overlap

The Skill stops at qualification. It does not write outreach, infer sensitive traits, scrape prohibited data, send messages, or mutate a CRM without a separate approved workflow.

## Evaluated alternatives

- Broad lead-generation Skills were rejected because they mix research, enrichment, copywriting, sending, and CRM writes under one trigger.
- A future account-intelligence Skill must own ongoing monitored signals, not duplicate one-time prospect discovery.
