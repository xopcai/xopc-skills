# Minimalist Review

Collect the review period, prior commitments, customer and product evidence, revenue and cash summary, acquisition and retention signals, delivery quality, time allocation, recurring risks and founder sustainability. Label every number by period, currency, source and confidence; reconcile conflicts rather than averaging them away.

Review five systems: customer problem and value, demand and sales, delivery and quality, economics and cash, and founder capacity and resilience. Compare plan with actual, identify meaningful changes and separate one-off events from repeatable patterns. Do not turn incomplete bookkeeping into financial certainty.

Create a keep/stop/start decision ledger. Rank constraints by customer harm, existential risk, economic impact, evidence strength and reversibility. Select one primary constraint and at most three commitments that fit real capacity; preserve a not-now list so strategy is expressed through exclusions.

Return the scorecard, evidence gaps, wins and failures, constraint statement, decisions, next-cycle commitments with measures, and risks requiring professional or external review. This Skill decides the business focus; use planning Skills later to schedule the work.
