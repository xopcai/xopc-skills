# Validate Idea

Restate the hypothesis as a named customer, recurring job, painful current state, existing workaround, promised outcome, offer and price assumption. Record what must be true and what evidence would disprove it. Opinions, likes and compliments are weak signals; recent behavior, effort, budget, deposits and completed purchases are stronger.

Design the smallest ethical evidence ladder: inspect existing behavior, conduct problem interviews without pitching, offer a manual or concierge solution, then ask for a proportionate commitment. Define sample, time box, success threshold and stop condition before collecting results. Do not fabricate interviewees, testimonials, demand or payment evidence, and do not contact anyone without authorization.

Maintain an evidence table for each assumption with observation, source, date, signal strength, contradictory evidence and confidence. Distinguish no evidence from negative evidence. Avoid universal numeric thresholds: interpret results using price, buying cycle, reachability and the cost of the next experiment.

Return a verdict of proceed, revise, gather evidence, or stop; the evidence behind it; the weakest assumption; and one bounded next experiment with cost, duration and decision rule. A proceed verdict authorizes learning work, not an unbounded build.
