# Company Values

Values for a one-person company are decision rules before they are culture copy. Gather concrete moments: a customer the founder would refuse, quality they would delay for, transparency they owe, work they will not automate, behavior they reward, and trade-offs made under time or cash pressure. Do not infer noble principles from aspirations alone.

Draft three to five principles using: name, decision rule, evidence story, expected behaviors, prohibited behaviors, customer promise, cost willingly accepted, and a boundary where the principle does not apply. Test each against two hard scenarios such as refund versus cash, speed versus quality, privacy versus growth, or revenue versus fit.

Remove duplicates, platitudes and principles that cannot change a decision. Check conflicts and precedence. Translate accepted principles into review questions for product, marketing, vendors, AI automation, collaborators and future hiring. Regulated policy, contracts and employment rules require qualified review.

Return the principle set, conflict tests, operating checklist, evidence still missing and a review date. Do not publish, update a handbook or communicate the values externally without explicit authorization.
