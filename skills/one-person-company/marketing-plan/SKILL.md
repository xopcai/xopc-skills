# Marketing Plan

Confirm business stage, audience, trigger, differentiated promise, proof, offer, price, sales motion, geography, language, founder strengths, weekly time and cash limits. Use current channel evidence where available; mark assumptions and do not copy competitors' claims or customer language without rights and context.

Choose one primary audience, one positioning statement, one conversion event and no more than two active acquisition channels. Select channels by audience presence, founder credibility, feedback speed, cost, shelf life and operational burden—not fashion. Define message pillars from observed customer questions, objections, outcomes and proof.

Design a four-week loop from research to asset, distribution, conversation, conversion and reuse. Specify cadence, owner, time budget, call to action, landing or sales handoff, attribution limits and leading and lagging metrics. Existing content-production Skills may create the assets; this Skill owns the system and trade-offs.

Return positioning, message map, channel decision, weekly calendar, experiment cards, measurement sheet and stop/continue rules. Publishing, paid spend, influencer contact, account access and external automation require explicit authorization. Prefer a small trusted audience and repeatable learning over vanity reach.
