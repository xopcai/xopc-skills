# Processize

Confirm the validation evidence, customer input, promised outcome, exclusions, service level, price hypothesis and maximum founder capacity. If demand or willingness to pay is still assumed, route back to idea validation rather than disguising product development as process design.

Map one delivery from trigger to acceptance: intake, qualification, consent and data handling, production steps, quality check, customer review, handoff, payment, support and deletion or retention. For every step record owner, input, output, tool, active time, elapsed time, failure mode and recovery. Use human checkpoints for irreversible, financial, public or sensitive actions.

Calculate contribution before founder salary, effective hourly return, weekly capacity, rework rate and the queue limit. Identify the single largest cost or variance source. Automate only a stable, repeated step with a measurable benefit; never automate uncertain judgment, consent, payment or publishing merely to appear scalable.

Return a runnable manual-delivery checklist, customer-facing promise, capacity and economics table, exception log, quality acceptance criteria, and the evidence threshold for automating one step. This is a learning process, not yet a mature organizational SOP.
