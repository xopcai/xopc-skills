# Grow Sustainably

Establish the current funnel and operating baseline: qualified demand, conversion, price, retention or repeat purchase, gross contribution, cash timing, delivery hours, support, defects, refunds, concentration and founder energy. State missing or unreliable data instead of manufacturing a growth rate.

Locate one binding constraint across reach, trust, conversion, activation, value delivery, retention, referral, price, capacity or cash. Trace symptoms to the constraint and test alternatives. More acquisition is harmful when fulfillment, retention or economics are broken.

Compare four levers: improve the system, narrow the offer, raise price or reduce low-value work; automate a stable step; delegate a documented and reviewable task; or deliberately cap growth. Evaluate customer impact, reversibility, cash exposure, founder time, quality controls and data access. Hiring and tooling are options, not default milestones.

Return the constraint diagnosis, evidence, a 30-day experiment, guardrail metrics, capacity and cash check, rollback trigger and a short not-now list. Never purchase tools, hire, change prices, contact customers or modify production systems without separate authorization.
