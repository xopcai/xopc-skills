# First Customers

Confirm the ideal early customer, trigger event, painful current workaround, offer, proof, price, capacity and learning goal. Define a cohort appropriate to the sales cycle rather than promising an arbitrary number of customers.

Build a small prospect map from authorized first-party relationships, referrals, community participation and separately researched public evidence. For each candidate record fit, observed trigger, relationship or lawful contact path, source, confidence and why now. Never invent personal details, harvest private data, bypass platform rules or treat purchased lists as consent.

Prepare a conversation flow: relevant observation, permission-based opener, problem questions, qualification, concise offer, objection evidence, next step and respectful close. Drafts must be individualized and reviewable. Sending messages, booking meetings, changing CRM records, offering discounts or committing delivery requires user approval.

Track attempts, replies, qualified conversations, proposals, paid conversions, loss reasons, time spent and delivery capacity. Return the prioritized cohort, outreach drafts, call guide, follow-up cadence, experiment ledger and a decision rule for changing customer, problem, offer, proof or channel. Optimize learning and trust before volume.
