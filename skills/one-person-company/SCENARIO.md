# One-person company

## User outcome

Turn one founder's limited time, cash and attention into an evidence-driven path from community and problem discovery to a profitable, repeatable business.

## Selected Skills

- `find-community` identifies a reachable community before choosing a product.
- `validate-idea` tests a problem and willingness to pay before implementation.
- `processize` designs a paid manual delivery loop before automation.
- `mvp` scopes the smallest product experiment after validation evidence exists.
- `pricing` builds a price and unit-economics hypothesis.
- `first-customers` runs founder-led sales for the first repeatable customer cohort.
- `marketing-plan` selects a narrow message, channel and measurable acquisition loop.
- `grow-sustainably` diagnoses the binding growth constraint without outrunning capacity or cash.
- `minimalist-review` reviews business health and chooses the next constraint to address.
- `company-values` converts founder principles into operating and delegation rules.

## Boundary

These Skills make uncertainty and assumptions explicit. They do not invent market evidence, guarantee revenue, send outreach, publish content, purchase tools, form a legal entity, provide regulated advice, or modify business systems without separate authorization. Adjacent general Skills may supply research, campaign assets, prospect evidence, SOPs or financial analysis, but do not own the OPC lifecycle decision.
