# Pricing

Confirm the customer, measurable outcome, unit of value, buying context, alternatives, delivery model, geography and currency. Gather current evidence for alternative prices and customer behavior; date volatile claims and distinguish list price from realized price and total cost.

Build a unit-economics worksheet with price, discounts, payment fees, variable service and infrastructure cost, support and rework, founder delivery time, acquisition cost assumption, refunds, tax treatment as an unknown unless supplied, contribution margin, capacity and cash timing. Do not hide founder labor merely because no salary is paid yet.

Generate at most three explainable packages around genuine differences in outcome, usage, service or risk—not artificial feature withholding. Check affordability, value capture, willingness-to-pay evidence, contribution, capacity and downside. Avoid invented precision, dark patterns, fake anchors and unsupported claims that a price is optimal.

Return recommended price or test range, package table, assumptions, sensitivity cases, customers needed for the stated income target, a reversible price experiment, and review triggers. Flag jurisdiction-specific tax, contract and consumer-law questions for qualified review.
