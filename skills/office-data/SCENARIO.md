# Office data

## User outcome

Turn spreadsheet files into trustworthy analysis, models, and carefully edited workbooks that remain usable in common office applications.

## Selected Skill

- `spreadsheet-workbench` owns XLSX/XLSM/CSV/TSV creation, analysis, editing, formulas, and validation.

## Boundary

It does not own databases, notebooks, or prose-only data summaries when no spreadsheet artifact is involved.
