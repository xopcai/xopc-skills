# Office data

## User outcome

Turn tabular evidence into a trustworthy workbook analysis or an accurate, accessible visualization.

## Selected Skills

- `spreadsheet-workbench` owns XLSX/XLSM/CSV/TSV creation, analysis, editing, formulas, and validation.
- `data-visualization-report` owns chart selection, data-to-visual validation, accessible rendering, and evidence-backed insight reporting.

## Boundary

General workbook operations remain in `spreadsheet-workbench`; a requested visual artifact with chart-specific validation belongs to `data-visualization-report`. Databases and notebooks remain outside this scenario.
