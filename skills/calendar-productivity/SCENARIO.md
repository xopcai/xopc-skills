# Calendar productivity

## User outcome

Understand a workday, prepare for an upcoming meeting, or find fair meeting times using calendar evidence without silently changing events.

## Selected Skills

- `daily-agenda-brief` owns one-day schedule understanding.
- `meeting-preparation` owns context and readiness for one upcoming meeting.
- `group-scheduling` owns multi-person time selection and tradeoffs.

## Boundary

Creating, rescheduling, cancelling, or editing events requires explicit confirmation. Post-meeting actions remain in `meeting-execution`.
