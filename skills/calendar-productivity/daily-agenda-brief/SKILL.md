# Daily Agenda Brief

Resolve the date, timezone, relevant calendars, and working hours. Query an explicit half-open day window in that timezone. Distinguish confirmed meetings from tentative holds, free blocks, travel, focus time, working elsewhere, and out-of-office status when the source exposes them.

Describe the shape of the day rather than dumping events. Highlight overlaps, missing transition time, unusually dense stretches, location changes, preparation needs, and meaningful free windows. Preserve event titles and times; if a shared calendar exposes only free/busy data, say so.

Return the date and timezone, a compact chronological agenda, conflict and logistics warnings, the best usable free windows, and what needs attention next. For today, emphasize remaining events; for future days, emphasize density and preparation. Do not create focus blocks, move events, or change RSVP state without explicit authorization.
