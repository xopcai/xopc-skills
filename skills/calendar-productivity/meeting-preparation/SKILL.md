# Meeting Preparation

Start from the selected event: purpose, time, timezone, organizer, attendees, response state, location or call link, description, attachments, and linked materials. Follow only relevant accessible links or nearby threads; broader research requires the user's request.

Separate confirmed context from inference. Identify the likely meeting outcome, decisions needed, stakeholder positions, prior commitments, unresolved questions, pre-reading, and what the user should prepare. Do not invent attendee views from titles or seniority. Surface missing context, access limitations, and stale materials.

Return a brief with meeting purpose, logistics, attendee relevance, key context, decisions or questions, recommended pre-read order, and a short “before the meeting” checklist. Updating the invitation, contacting attendees, or creating follow-ups is separate from preparation and requires authorization.
