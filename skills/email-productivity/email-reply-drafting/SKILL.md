# Email Reply Drafting

Identify the exact message or thread and read the latest ask plus enough history to understand participants, decisions, commitments, dates, tone, and unresolved questions. Distinguish reply from reply-all based on who needs the shared context; do not copy recipients merely because they appeared earlier.

Draft the shortest response that fully answers the ask. Preserve names, dates, links, quoted facts, decisions, and existing commitments. Do not invent approval, availability, ownership, attachments, or promises. If essential information is missing, use a clearly marked placeholder or list the unresolved detail after the draft rather than fabricating it.

Return subject handling if needed, recommended recipients, a ready-to-send draft, assumptions or placeholders, and one-line reply-all reasoning when relevant. Default to draft only. Before sending, recheck final recipients, attachments, sensitive content, and the exact body, then obtain confirmation unless the user already authorized that precise message.
