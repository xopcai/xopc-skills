# Email productivity

## User outcome

Understand a bounded mailbox slice and prepare accurate replies without silently changing mailbox state or sending messages.

## Selected Skills

- `inbox-triage` owns attention ranking and next-action classification.
- `email-reply-drafting` owns a grounded response to one selected message or thread.

## Boundary

Triage does not draft every reply; reply drafting does not audit the inbox. Sending, moving, deleting, categorizing, or marking mail requires explicit authorization.
