# User research

## User outcome

Turn mixed qualitative and quantitative research evidence into traceable patterns, opportunity areas, and testable product decisions.

## Selected Skills

- `user-research-synthesis` owns research-corpus preparation, coding, triangulation, segmentation, insight formation, and evidence-linked opportunity framing.

## Boundary

Questionnaire construction remains in `form-survey-builder`, product-decision experiments in `decision-prototype`, and general cross-workspace lookup in `workspace-knowledge-synthesis`. This scenario does not fabricate prevalence from small qualitative samples.
