# Career support

## User outcome

Present a candidate's verified experience for a specific opportunity without fabrication, discriminatory inference, or misleading keyword stuffing.

## Selected Skills

- `resume-tailoring` owns evidence inventory, JD mapping, truthful accomplishment rewriting, gap disclosure, and content handoff for a resume or CV.

## Boundary

Document rendering remains in `document-authoring`; hiring pipeline analysis remains in `recruiting-pipeline-review`. This scenario supports the candidate and does not impersonate employers, submit applications, or guarantee selection.
