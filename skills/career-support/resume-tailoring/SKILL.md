# Resume Tailoring

Confirm the target role, region and language, job description, seniority, page or format constraints, and the candidate's source resume. Build an evidence inventory of employers, dates, responsibilities, tools, scope, outcomes, and metrics before rewriting.

Map each job requirement to verified evidence, transferable evidence, or a genuine gap. Prioritize relevant experience and translate internal jargon, but never create an employer, title, date, certification, technology, responsibility, team size, or result. Mark metrics that the candidate must confirm.

Rewrite bullets around context, action, scope, and outcome. Use concrete nouns and verbs, preserve nuance, and include keywords only where the underlying experience supports them. Avoid hidden text, keyword stuffing, inflated seniority, and sensitive personal attributes not required for the application.

Deliver a tailored content draft, requirement-to-evidence matrix, changes requiring candidate confirmation, remaining gaps, and optional interview evidence prompts. Keep a machine-readable structure and hand off to a document Skill only when layout or export is requested. Do not submit or publish without explicit approval.
