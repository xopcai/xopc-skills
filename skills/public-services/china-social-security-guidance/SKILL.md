# China Social Security Guidance

Confirm city or province, date, employment status, insurance type, contribution history, household or residency facts only when relevant, and the exact decision or calculation requested. Minimize identity numbers and account details.

Browse by default because rules, bases, rates, retirement arrangements, and application channels change. Prefer current national and local human-resources/social-security, medical-insurance, housing-fund, tax, and government-service sources. Record jurisdiction, effective date, publication date, and link; resolve national versus local rules explicitly.

For calculations, show the formula, contribution base, rates, caps/floors, months, rounding, and every assumption. Separate employee, employer, and flexible-employment treatment. Provide ranges when future parameters or credited years are unknown.

Deliver a plain-language answer, dated source table, calculation when possible, missing facts, official verification channel, required-material checklist, and questions to ask the agency. Do not guarantee eligibility, benefit amount, retirement date, reimbursement, subsidy, or approval, and do not submit or change enrollment without explicit authorization.
