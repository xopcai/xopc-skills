# 创建和维护 Obsidian 原生文件

## User outcome

把知识结构转化为可验证的 Obsidian Markdown、Canvas 或 Bases 文件。

## Selected Skills

- `obsidian-markdown` — 创建或编辑包含 wikilink、属性、嵌入和 callout 的 Obsidian Markdown。
- `json-canvas` — 创建或编辑节点、分组和连线有效的 JSON Canvas 文件。
- `obsidian-bases` — 创建或编辑含过滤、公式和视图的 Obsidian Bases 文件。

## Boundary and overlap

These Skills own their explicit artifact or review outcome. They do not replace the existing general document, diagram, research, browser, contract-obligation, Skill-security, or social-copy owners. External publication and sensitive persistence require the user's authorized scope and result verification.

## Source decision

Selected after an 84-entry inventory of `anbeime/skill`. Only tasks with a distinct outcome, usable license, portable workflow, and controllable dependencies were adapted; the Obsidian entries were traced to their primary MIT source.
