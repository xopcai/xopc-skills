# Travel Itinerary Planning

Confirm origin, destinations, dates and flexibility, travelers, budget and currency, interests, pace, mobility or dietary needs, accommodation status, transport preferences, passport or residency context, and must-do or must-avoid items. State assumptions when the user prefers a first draft.

## Verify volatile facts

When internet access exists, check official or primary sources for opening days, reservation rules, transport schedules, local holidays, entry requirements, weather alerts, and material prices. Record the checked date and link. Treat blog and social recommendations as discovery signals, not authority for safety, border, or schedule facts.

Never promise visa approval, entry, availability, safety, or a fixed price. Direct travelers to the relevant government, carrier, venue, and insurance sources for final confirmation.

## Construct a feasible route

Anchor fixed bookings and hard constraints first. Cluster activities geographically, include realistic transfer, queue, meal, rest, and check-in time, and avoid scheduling every day at maximum capacity. Match the pace to children, older travelers, accessibility needs, jet lag, altitude, climate, and luggage.

For each day provide time window, place or activity, why it fits, expected duration, transfer method and time, reservation status, cost range, and a weather or closure fallback. Make arrival and departure days deliberately light.

## Build the trip controls

Separate confirmed costs, estimates, and optional upgrades. Show transport, lodging, food, admission, local travel, insurance, connectivity, and contingency ranges without silently converting currencies. Identify booking deadlines, cancellation terms to verify, and dependencies between reservations.

Deliver:

- a trip summary and key assumptions;
- a day-by-day route that can be followed;
- budget ranges by category and traveler;
- booking and document checklist with deadlines;
- accessibility, health, connectivity, payment, and local-transport notes;
- fallback plans for weather, closure, delay, fatigue, and overspend;
- a final fact-check list with sources and checked dates.

Do not purchase, reserve, cancel, or submit personal data without explicit authorization and a final review of price and terms. Minimize passport, identity, health, and payment details in the working output.
