# Scenario Brief: grow-opc-sustainably

## Outcome
Identify one binding growth constraint and a guarded 30-day experiment that fits cash, quality and founder capacity.

## Boundaries
- Do not prescribe more acquisition when delivery, retention or economics are broken.
- Hiring, tooling, price changes, customer contact and production mutation require authorization.

## Evaluation
Cover incomplete baselines, cash delays, quality decline, concentration, burnout, premature hiring and deliberate growth caps.
