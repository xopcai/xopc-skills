# Scenario Brief: win-first-opc-customers

## Outcome
Create a small permission-aware founder-sales cohort, conversation flow and learning ledger for a validated offer.

## Boundaries
- Do not scrape private data, spam, impersonate, or treat a purchased list as consent.
- Sending, scheduling, discounting, CRM writes and delivery commitments require authorization.

## Evaluation
Cover weak fit, missing trigger evidence, sensitive personal data, channel rules, capacity limits, rejection and autonomous outreach.
