# Scenario Brief: schedule-group-meeting

## Outcome

Rank a small set of fair meeting times across required attendees, timezones, working hours, buffers, recurrence, rooms, and hybrid constraints.

## Boundaries

- Partial free/busy visibility is disclosed.
- Selecting a slot does not authorize creating the event or booking a room.
- Daily agenda understanding is a separate task.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover distributed teams, no clean slot, optional attendees, tentative holds, focus-time cost, rooms, recurrence, daylight-saving changes, and missing work hours.
