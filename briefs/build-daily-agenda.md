# Scenario Brief: build-daily-agenda

## Outcome

Turn one calendar day into a timezone-aware agenda explaining conflicts, density, logistics, preparation, and usable free windows.

## Boundaries

- No event mutation or focus-block creation without authorization.
- Free, tentative, working-elsewhere, and out-of-office are not equivalent.
- In-depth preparation for one meeting remains separate.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover overlaps, travel, multi-calendar visibility, partial free/busy, all-day status, timezone edges, remaining-today events, and empty days.
