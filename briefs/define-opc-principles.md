# Scenario Brief: define-opc-principles

## Outcome
Convert observed founder trade-offs into three to five operating principles that change real decisions.

## Boundaries
- Reject slogans and invented origin stories.
- Do not publish, change handbooks or substitute values for employment, contract or regulated policy.

## Evaluation
Cover conflicting principles, privacy versus growth, revenue versus fit, absent stories, platitudes, AI delegation and unauthorized publication.
