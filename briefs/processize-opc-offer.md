# Scenario Brief: processize-opc-offer

## Outcome
Turn a validated offer into a runnable paid manual delivery loop with quality, exception, capacity and economics controls.

## Boundaries
- Route unvalidated demand back to validation and mature operations documentation to SOP authoring.
- Keep consent, sensitive data, payments, publishing and irreversible steps under human control.

## Evaluation
Cover missing acceptance criteria, hidden founder labor, sensitive intake, rework, queue overload, unstable steps and premature automation.
