# Scenario Brief: find-opc-community

## Outcome
Identify one to three communities the founder can authentically reach, with dated evidence of recurring valuable problems and a bounded learning plan.

## Boundaries
- Do not equate audience size with community quality or invent people and pain points.
- Do not access private spaces, scrape restricted member data, or recommend deceptive participation.

## Evaluation
Cover weak founder access, demographic-only segments, private groups, conflicting pain evidence, no spending signal and premature product proposals.
