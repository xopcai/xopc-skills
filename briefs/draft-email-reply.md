# Scenario Brief: draft-email-reply

## Outcome

Draft a concise response grounded in one selected thread, with accurate recipients, facts, commitments, dates, links, tone, and unresolved details.

## Boundaries

- Inbox-wide prioritization and new outbound marketing are separate.
- Drafting never implies sending authorization.
- Approval, availability, attachments, and promises are not invented.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover reply-all ambiguity, missing facts, stale threads, commitments, sensitive recipients, attachments, tone changes, and explicit send requests.
