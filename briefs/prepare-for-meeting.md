# Scenario Brief: prepare-for-meeting

## Outcome

Build a focused pre-meeting brief from the invitation, attendees, linked materials, prior commitments, decisions, and open questions.

## Boundaries

- Attendee positions are not inferred from role or seniority.
- Broader research and invite edits require separate scope.
- Post-meeting action extraction remains separate.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover sparse invites, inaccessible links, stale documents, conflicting decisions, partial RSVP state, repeated meetings, and last-minute changes.
