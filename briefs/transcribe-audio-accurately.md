# Scenario Brief: transcribe-audio-accurately

## Outcome

Transcribe supplied audio or video into a faithful, reviewable transcript with timestamps, speaker labels, uncertainty markers, and privacy-aware handling.

## Boundaries

- One primary result and a bounded evidence scope.
- Preserve source material and distinguish facts, uncertainty, and assumptions.
- Paid calls, uploads, rights-sensitive use, publishing, and destructive actions require authorization.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover missing consent, uncertain evidence, unavailable tools, accessibility, rights, and adjacent Skill requests.
