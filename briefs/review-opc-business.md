# Scenario Brief: review-opc-business

## Outcome
Turn a review period's business evidence into one constraint, at most three feasible commitments and a not-now list.

## Boundaries
- Do not convert incomplete bookkeeping into accounting certainty.
- Keep scheduling in planning Skills and regulated conclusions with qualified professionals.

## Evaluation
Cover conflicting metrics, one-off revenue, incomplete cash data, too many priorities, founder health, prior commitments and unsupported optimism.
