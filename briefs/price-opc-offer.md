# Scenario Brief: price-opc-offer

## Outcome
Produce an explainable price or range, unit economics, sensitivity cases and a reversible willingness-to-pay experiment.

## Boundaries
- Do not hide founder labor, invent optimality, or use deceptive pricing.
- Treat tax, accounting, contracts and consumer law as jurisdiction-specific professional questions.

## Evaluation
Cover mixed currencies, list versus realized price, negative contribution, service capacity, discounts, refunds and uncertain tax.
