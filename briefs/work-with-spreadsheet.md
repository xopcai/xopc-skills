# Scenario Brief: work-with-spreadsheet

## Outcome

Analyze, create, edit, repair, or validate a spreadsheet while preserving workbook features and reconciling formulas and key totals.

## Boundaries

- Existing workbooks are edited conservatively and never silently rebuilt.
- Macro, pivot, chart, and external-link limitations are disclosed.
- Databases and notebooks use their own scenarios.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover XLSX, XLSM, CSV, formulas, hidden sheets, named ranges, pivots, chart references, format preservation, and recalculation.
