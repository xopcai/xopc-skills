# Scenario Brief: plan-opc-marketing

## Outcome
Create a focused four-week acquisition system with one audience, one conversion and no more than two active channels.

## Boundaries
- Own positioning and channel trade-offs, while content Skills own asset production.
- Publishing, spend, account access, influencer contact and automation require authorization.

## Evaluation
Cover vanity metrics, channel overload, absent proof, copied claims, tiny founder capacity, attribution gaps and unauthorized publishing.
