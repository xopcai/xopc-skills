# Scenario Brief: draft-internal-communication

## User and outcome

Teams need concise updates that turn verified operating facts into the right message for employees, leaders, or incident stakeholders.

## Boundaries

- Drafting does not authorize publication or impersonation.
- Unknown causes, impact, owners, and dates remain unknown.
- Public marketing and release notes use their dedicated scenarios.

## Evaluation

Fixtures include mixed-status projects, sensitive incidents, leadership decisions, FAQs with pending policy, and newsletters containing low-value activity noise.
