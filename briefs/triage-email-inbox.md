# Scenario Brief: triage-email-inbox

## Outcome

Rank a bounded mailbox slice by attention and next action with evidence, confidence, and transparent coverage.

## Boundaries

- Reply-needed status is an inference, not guaranteed truth.
- Triage does not draft every reply or mutate mailbox state.
- A limited query cannot support an “inbox zero” claim.

## Evaluation

Fixtures cover direct asks, deadlines, waiting threads, newsletters, automated alerts, ambiguous ownership, partial thread history, and narrow scopes.
