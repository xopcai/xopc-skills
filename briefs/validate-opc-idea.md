# Scenario Brief: validate-opc-idea

## Outcome
Reach a proceed, revise, gather-evidence or stop decision for a problem and offer using a predeclared ethical evidence ladder.

## Boundaries
- Do not treat compliments, survey intent or fabricated demand as payment evidence.
- Do not contact prospects, take deposits or begin an unbounded build without authorization.

## Evaluation
Cover contradictory interviews, long buying cycles, no workaround, biased samples, deposits, negative evidence and unclear stop rules.
